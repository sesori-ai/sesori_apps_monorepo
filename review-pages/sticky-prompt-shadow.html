The choice

One thing worth knowing before you pick

Once the full-width fade is gone, the bubble's own opaque box is the only thing covering rows — and its bottom edge lands mid-line. The bottom halves of those letters keep showing, which reads as a row of broken glyphs pressed against the bubble. You can see it in option A as “Older vaults keep ·····” trailing into rubble.

A page-coloured halo hides exactly that rubble and nothing else. A dark shadow leaves the rubble legible and only adds depth on top of it. That is the real difference between B and C/D — not the amount of elevation.
