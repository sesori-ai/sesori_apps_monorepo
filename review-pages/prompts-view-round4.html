Correction to round 3, Q2

I said six harnesses (Grok, Antigravity, Copilot, Cursor, Hermes, OMP) have no prompt time, so there was nothing truthful to show. That is true of their protocol — but it is not the whole picture. The bridge already knows when it accepted your prompt, and deliberately throws that number away. There is a hook for exactly this (a backend-authoritative time for a locally accepted user message); the six harnesses leave it returning null.

So giving them times is a small bridge change, not the separate project I implied. You picked "no times" partly on my bad cost estimate, which is why it is back.
