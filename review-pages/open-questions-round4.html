Open questions, round 4

Three gaps from Codex's later rounds that I shouldn't close for you. Two are on the instant-new-session plan (#1774), one is on the Prompts screen plan (#1804). All other findings in those rounds are settled.

1. The real session can show up before the app knows it belongs to the placeholder

After you press Send, the list shows a "Creating…" row until the bridge answers. The plan promises that row is swapped in place for the real one, so you never see two rows for one launch.

The bridge documents that its "session created" broadcast can arrive before its reply to the create request. In that gap the app sees a brand-new session and doesn't yet know it's the one it's waiting for. For a moment, then, the list could show both the placeholder and the real row, and one of them would then disappear. That's a jump. The gap is usually milliseconds, so it would be rare, but it would be visible when it happens.

2. The composer disappears for a moment after Send

The plan promises that after you press Send on a new session, the composer stays usable the whole time, so you can keep typing a follow-up. But the session screen only builds its composer after its first load finishes. When the new-session screen hands over to the session screen, the composer is gone for up to about 2.5 s. If you were typing, the keyboard drops and the composer disappears and comes back. Today every session opens this way, but here it lands right after Send, on the one flow this plan exists to make feel instant.

3. Closing the Prompts screen with the iPhone back swipe

The Prompts screen is a layer over the session, not a new page, as decided earlier, so the transcript underneath never moves. The side effect: while the layer is open, iOS's edge swipe from the left does nothing. The plan currently closes it only by tapping a prompt or the close control. Android's back gesture works either way.

Anything else

Three things decided without asking (nothing to pick; say so if you disagree)

  • "Creating…" waits at most one extra update. A placeholder is kept until the real session appears where it will sit, such as in Activity. If your first message is a command that never runs, the session may never show up in Activity, and holding strictly would leave "Creating…" on screen forever. The limit has a cost: an unrelated update in the same project can release the placeholder one update early.
  • Sidebar counts include "Creating…" rows, so the Activity header never reads 0 above a visible row.
  • Phone home search hides "Creating…" rows, matching the session list's existing rule (your D4).
  • Prompts screen: an undated prompt keeps its place. A prompt with no time, later in the list, gets its own "No date" header where it sits, instead of being pulled into one group at the top. This follows your "keep the transcript's order" decision.
  • Prompts screen: no transition between two PRs. Between steps 11 and 12 of that series, the Prompts button on main opens the screen without its transition. It's only an interim state inside the series, not something that would ship in a release.
