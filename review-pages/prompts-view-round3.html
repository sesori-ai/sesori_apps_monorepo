Prompts view — round 3

Two things you raised in round 2: can the numbers on the spine be trusted, and should the header line be a search field that shows a longer excerpt for matches. I went and checked what the bridge and the app actually know today. Rounds 1 and 2 are answered and settled; nothing below re-asks them.

Already settled (no choice needed)

  • A pinch opens a separate Prompts screen; the transcript itself never folds or relayouts.
  • Dense rows, deliberately unlike the chat: no bubbles.
  • D4 spine: a dot on a vertical line, the prompt, the time.
  • One line per prompt, ellipsised; sticky day headers; tap jumps back to that prompt.
  • A filter field at the top — this round decides what it searches and how matches look.

Questions

Q1Can the spine be numbered honestly?Pick one

Short answer: not today. Nothing in the app or the bridge knows that a prompt is "the 12th". The app only holds the pages it has loaded (50 messages at a time), so numbering what is loaded means every "load earlier" renumbers every row you already read — and not even by a fixed amount, because the oldest turn boundary can move when the previous page arrives. Numbers that change while you look at them are worse than no numbers.

Q2Six harnesses never tell us when you sent a promptPick one

The time on the right, and the day headers, come from the prompt's own timestamp. Claude, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and DeepSeek send one. Grok, Antigravity, Copilot, Cursor, Hermes and OMP do not — their protocol carries no time with a prompt. On those sessions there is nothing truthful to put in that column.

Q3Does the search reach prompts that are not loaded yet?Pick one

There is no prompt-search route in the bridge today, and no search index in its database. The app can only filter what it has already pulled down — a long session loads 50 messages at a time as you scroll up.

Q4Does the field replace the header line?Pick one

Round 2 settled a header line ("Prompts · 9 in this session") and, separately, a filter field. Your note asked whether the field should simply be the header.

Q5Showing the match when it falls past the one-line cutPick one

Rows are one ellipsised line. If your word appears late in a long prompt, a plain one-line row shows none of it — the row matches and you cannot see why.

Facts behind this round — nothing to pick

  • No numbering exists anywhere today. Messages carry a per-session sequence number internally, but it counts every message including the agent's, so it is not a prompt number.
  • The bridge does not know what a "turn" is. Grouping a prompt with the work that followed it is a rule in the app. That is why a bridge-side count can only answer "your Nth message".
  • Numbers can still go stale if a session is re-imported or the harness rewrites its own history (a rewind or an edited message). Worst case is a wrong number on an old row — cosmetic, so no machinery to defend it.
  • Loading the whole transcript up front would make both numbering and search free, but it pulls the entire session over the wire — the opposite of the payload work you asked for today. Not on the table.
  • Everything else a row shows — prompt text, time, step count, duration, failure, running — already exists in the app. Only "Stopped" does not, and that needs option C in Q1.
