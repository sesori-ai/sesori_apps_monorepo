Desktop: timestamp alignment and text width

The misaligned, clipped timestamp is fixed — that was a bug, nothing to choose. The text width is yours to pick, and it is genuinely coupled to the timestamps, so the renders below show both together. Every shot keeps the composer and the pills at their current width, as you asked.

The timestamp bug — fixed, nothing to pick

Why it happened: every transcript row is shrink-wrapped to its own content, so the timestamp's gutter was anchored to each message's right edge rather than to the reading column. A short "Done." revealed its time mid-column; a full-width paragraph revealed it at the column edge; rows that filled the column got the label sliced at an invisible boundary. Measured before the fix: the same label landed at x = 360, 500 and 680 for three different message widths. After: 680 for all three.

The same bug exists on the phone, where it is nearly invisible because the column is the screen. One fix covers both; the drag, distance, spring-back and timing are untouched.

How wide should the text be?

Two things to know before picking

  • The transcript and the composer currently share one number (760). Widening the text without widening the composer means splitting that into two — about ten lines. Fine, just not free, and every render below already does it.
  • The timestamp gutter needs room. It travels 108 px out of the column, so it stays fully inside the window while text width ≤ window − 216. On a 1240-wide window that ceiling is 1024. Past it the peek degrades to the phone's behaviour — content slides under the window edge during the drag — rather than breaking, and a settled timestamp is never clipped at any width.
